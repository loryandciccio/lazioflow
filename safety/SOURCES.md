# Deterministic safety rules: references

The 38 configured rules are conservative software guardrails, not a validated clinical decision system. They inspect the original text, may over-trigger on negated descriptions, and cannot establish that an unflagged request is medically safe. Hard flags and uncertainty handling remain separate from facility ranking.

References inherited from the academic project are listed below. Original verification dates are retained in the JSON as historical metadata, not evidence of a fresh verification. External quotations are not reproduced in this edition. One precautionary self-harm guardrail lacks a recorded institutional verification date; qualified review is required before real healthcare use.
